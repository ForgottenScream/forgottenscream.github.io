## Overview

This was my final research project at university. This was my implementation for
a research paper I wrote.

## Motivation

GDPR enforcement is still very hard to analyse across different jurisdictions,
this pipeline aims to simplify the process.


## Challenges

There were quite a few challenges, mainly the enforcementtracker.com website
updating to V2 and having the exact same statistics and graphs as I had done.
Also more Data Protection Authorities must be added to produce better analysis.
